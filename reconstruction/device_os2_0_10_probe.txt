## Latest failed boot sequence

The latest ramoops includes the rebuilt g5987d69e25da boot and reaches Android first-stage init. It is not an immediate boot-header or ARM64 Image rejection.

Relevant sequence:
- logical partition system_a is created as dm-3
- logical partitions for the inactive _b slot are skipped because they have zero length
- first-stage init then queries system_b
- DM_DEV_STATUS for system_b fails
- /system mount fails
- InitFatalReboot is issued

The same log also reports that /proc/bootconfig is absent.

The proxy-consumer.ko module_layout warning is not by itself the fatal blocker: historical stock g4c23b1a30923 sessions in the same persistent log show the same warning and continue booting.

## Reconstruction rule

Do not produce another flash candidate from the OS2.0.16 g598 baseline for this device.

First recover and verify the exact OS2.0.10.0.UKOCNXM stock boot baseline. The official baseline must match the live OS2.0.10 vendor_boot/dtbo evidence before kernel ABI reconstruction and boot repacking continue.
